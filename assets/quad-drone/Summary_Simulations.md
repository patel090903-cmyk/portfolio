## Design findings worth acting on
- **Blade-pass resonance risk (04):** at hover the 2P blade-pass frequency (192 Hz) sits within 0.1 % of the 192 Hz frame mode (strain energy: motor pad joint 66 %, arms 30 %, legs 4 %) = the motor pods rocking on the 5 mm pad cantilevered past the arm box end -> extend the box under the motor or thicken the pad (the root-clamped solid FE puts the same pod-pitch mode at 202 Hz). Several arm/pad/leg/mast modes cluster at ~185-200 Hz, so confirm with a tap test before changing parts.
- **1P (rotor imbalance) margin (04):** the nearest mode to hover 1P (96 Hz) is 103 Hz (+7.4 %) = the flight controller rocking on its silicone dampers (stiffness ASSUMED) -> softer dampers so every FC mount mode is well below 1P; it would AMPLIFY 1P vibration at the IMU about 4.4x (damping ratio 0.1 assumed). A heavier payload raises hover 1P (+2.7 Hz per 100 g) toward it. Balance the props and check the real damper data.
- **FC vibration isolation (04, ASSUMED damper stiffness 2 N/mm axial, 1 N/mm shear per ball):** FC-on-damper modes 29, 36, 51, 66, 68, 103 Hz; 2 of 6 isolate hover 1P well (transmissibility < 0.3), the others pass 0.4x, 0.9x, 1.0x, 4.4x of it. Softer dampers (all FC mount modes below ~35 Hz) would isolate 1P properly - check with the real damper data.
- **Arm download (03):** the arms and legs under the props cost about 5.5 % of rotor thrust in hover, i.e. ~9 % more hover power (included in the 02/05 hover power).
- **Drag is probably over-estimated (03):** the coarse 3-D RANS of the simplified airframe gives CdA 0.020 m^2 level and 0.024 m^2 at 15 deg nose-down, at or below the low end of the build-up (0.020-0.056 m^2 level). 02/05 keep the conservative nominal drag, so forward-flight range is more likely near the low-drag value (15.4 km) than the nominal 11.8 km.
- **Single motor failure (02):** with a standard PX4-style cascade the vehicle loses attitude control in 0.38 s and hits the ground 3.1 s after the failure from 30 m at 16 m/s. Surviving it needs a fault-tolerant (yaw-spinning, reduced-attitude) controller or a hexacopter layout - not implemented here.
- **Endurance (02/05):** 15.3 min hover to 80 % depth of discharge (17.8 min to a 3.5 V/cell loaded cutoff); best endurance is in slow forward flight (18.8 min at 8 m/s) because of translational lift.

## Resume-ready bullets (all simulated - say so)
- Built a 6-DOF quadrotor flight simulator in Python (quaternion rigid body, CAD-derived inertia, bench-fitted motor/ESC/battery models, BEMT rotor aerodynamics) with a PX4-style cascaded PID; verified hover stability by linearisation (48 deg phase / 21 dB gain margin) and simulated missions, 8 m/s wind with gusts, motor failure and battery-sag endurance (15.3 min hover).
- Modelled the T-Motor AIR2216/T1045 propulsion with blade-element momentum theory (NeuralFoil polars) validated against UIUC wind-tunnel data and fitted a motor/ESC model to bench data (electrical power within 1.2 % RMS).
- Ran SU2 RANS/URANS CFD: validated on a square cylinder (Cd 2.04 vs 2.05-2.2 measured), then computed the arm section drag in the rotor downwash (Cd 1.96) to estimate a 5.5 % thrust loss from rotor-arm interference; estimated airframe drag area from CAD projected areas and a 3-D RANS of a simplified airframe.
- Performed modal analysis of the 3D-printed PA6-CF frame (scikit-fem P2 FEA + calibrated free-free beam model) and a gyroscopic Campbell diagram; identified a blade-pass resonance risk at hover (+0.1 % separation) and proposed design changes.
- Swept endurance/range vs payload, airspeed and wind (best range 11.8 km at 13 m/s, TWR >= 2 up to 500 g payload).

## Not done / limits
- PX4 SITL: skipped - needs a WSL Linux distro + PX4 toolchain (multi-GB download); no WSL distro is installed and installing one was out of scope.
- Full-vehicle CFD with rotating props (OpenFOAM/SU2 with actuator disks or sliding mesh) was not run - see 03 README for what to run.
- No physical test data for this airframe: motor data are the manufacturer's bench table; damper stiffness, battery resistance and the GPS-mast base compliance are assumptions (ranges given in the READMEs).
